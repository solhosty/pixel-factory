The two-room proof in `docs/design/prototype/modular-map.html` remains historical evidence for shared layers, collision, and keyboard movement. The later approved product direction supersedes its doorway expansion: use one company-wide, curated Studio with six to eight local workstations and remove the adjoining Quiet workroom. Projects no longer own rooms. Reusable floor/wall/prop layers remain required; do not generate a bespoke illustrated room frame or procedurally append low-quality office space.

`warm`, `cool`, and `editorial` are user-controlled map presentation preferences over identical map data. They must not change exits, collisions, actors, task state, or the shared A-style application chrome. Persist the preference only after 1A has established its domain storage.

The UI/art baseline is A: deep navy framing, cream conversation surfaces, walnut/moss/terracotta accents, readable browser text, and restrained pixel-RPG framing. Do not substitute the concept screenshot as production UI. Decision interactions must accept custom user input and support revision history; a suggested choice begins a revision, never implicit approval. Menu frames and newly opened speech bubbles may have one brief settle animation, with a complete reduced-motion alternative and no looping/reading-time motion.

The original editable appearance-recipe requirement is superseded for the initial release. Employees select unique predefined complete character identities and customize only their names. Current candidate assets are provisional; preserve their manifest status and do not claim E03 or asset approval from their existence.
